Pick a standard or live voice, save a voice preference, or create a voice clone with Fish Audio where enabled.

Review call history, transcripts and outcomes. Contact-specific memories can help carry context into future conversations when enabled.

Retry a call with the right contact and objective. Call plans for phone calls require your confirmation.
