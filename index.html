Help & FAQ

How does Emysa make a call?

Type what you need done in plain language — like "call this number and ask if they have a table for four tonight" — and Emysa places a real phone call, has the conversation for you, and reports back what happened.

What is voice cloning for?

Recording a short 10–30 second sample lets the AI caller speak in a voice that sounds like yours instead of a generic voice. It's entirely optional — the AI caller can also use a library voice.

Is my call recorded?

Recording and transcription may be enabled to let you review what happened on a call. You're responsible for following your local law on notifying anyone you call that the conversation may be recorded — see the Privacy Policy for details.

Do I have a limit on how many calls I can make?

Your account has a monthly minute allowance shown in the app. Once you're close to the limit you'll be notified before any call that would put you over it.

How do I change my profile picture?

Open Profile and tap the small camera icon on your avatar to choose a photo from your library.

How do I delete my voice, my call history, or my whole account?

Go to Profile → Data controls. You can request deletion of any of these at any time.

Something isn't working — how do I reach support?

Email support@emysa.app with a description of the issue and, if it's about a specific call, roughly when it happened.

Get Started

1. Add a caller

From the Home tab, tell the AI who to call and what to say — like "call this number and ask about a table for four."

2. Clone your voice (optional)

Go to Profile → Voice and record 10–30 seconds so the AI caller can sound like you instead of a generic voice.

3. Check the outcome

Once the call ends, open the Recent tab to see what happened and read back what was said.
